## 1. About Brightmoor Kinetics

Brightmoor Kinetics builds warehouse robots that pick and pack small parcels. The company was founded
on 14 September 2017 in Tallinn, Estonia, by Mireille Okonkwo-Varga (Chief Executive Officer) and
Tobias Lindqvist (Chief Technology Officer). Our first product, the **Heron** picking arm, shipped in
2019. The current flagship is the **Kestrel K7** autonomous mobile robot, released in May 2025, which
carries up to 42 kg and runs for 11 hours on a single charge.

As of January 2026 Brightmoor employs 318 people across three offices and a remote workforce in
nine countries. Our internal mission statement is "Every parcel, one fewer touch." Our three
company values, in order, are **Candour**, **Craft**, and **Care**. When values conflict, Candour wins:
we would rather ship bad news early than good news late.

The fiscal year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Company-wide planning, which we call **Compass Week**,
happens in the second week of February every year. All-hands meetings take place on the first
Thursday of every month at 15:00 Tallinn time.

## 3. Working Hours and Hybrid Work

Brightmoor uses a hybrid model called **Anchor Days**. Employees assigned to an office are expected
on site on **Tuesdays and Thursdays** (the anchor days). The other three weekdays are flexible. Fully
remote employees, who are hired as remote from the start, have no anchor days but must visit an
office at least twice a year; the company pays for these trips.

Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time**. Outside core hours people
may choose their own schedule, as long as the team agreement is respected. The standard working
week is 38 hours for full-time employees.

Employees may work from another country for up to **30 working days per calendar year** under the
**Work From Anywhere** policy. Requests must be submitted in the HR portal, **Harbour**, at least
15 working days before travel and approved by both the manager and People Operations. Working from
Russia, Belarus, or any country under EU sanctions is never permitted, and Robotics Test Lab staff
are not eligible for Work From Anywhere because their work requires physical hardware.

## 4. Time Off and Leave

### 4.1 Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **27 days of paid annual leave** per fiscal year, in addition to the
public holidays of their country of employment. After **five full years** of service the allowance
rises to 30 days. Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next fiscal year; carried-over
days expire on **30 June**. Leave is requested in Harbour, and requests longer than 5 consecutive
working days need at least 4 weeks' notice.

### 4.2 Recharge Week

In addition to annual leave, the whole company closes for **Recharge Week**, which is always the
last full week of August. In 2026 Recharge Week runs from **24 to 28 August**. A small on-call
rotation keeps customer-facing systems running; people on that rotation get the same number of
days off in September instead, plus a one-off bonus of **€400**.

### 4.3 Sick leave

Employees can self-certify sick leave for up to **3 consecutive days**. From the fourth day a
doctor's note is required and must be uploaded to Harbour. Brightmoor tops up statutory sick pay
to **100% of base salary for the first 20 working days** of sickness in a rolling 12-month period.

### 4.4 Parental leave

Brightmoor offers **20 weeks of fully paid parental leave** to all parents, regardless of gender,
including adoptive and surrogate parents. This is on top of any statutory entitlement and can be
taken in up to three blocks within 18 months of the birth or adoption. On return, parents may work
a **four-day week at full pay for the first 8 weeks**, a programme we call **Soft Landing**.

### 4.5 Volunteering

Every employee has **2 paid volunteering days** per fiscal year for registered non-profit
organisations. Volunteering days are logged in Harbour under the leave type "Community".

All expenses are submitted in **Ledgerline**, our expense tool, within **30 days** of the purchase.
Receipts must be photographed and attached; handwritten receipts are accepted only for amounts under
€25. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

Business travel is booked through **Tern Travel**, our travel agency portal. Flights under 6 hours

### 5.3 Home-office budget

New employees receive a one-time **€900 home-office budget** for a desk, chair, monitor, or similar
equipment, and a further **€300 every three years** after that. Claims go through Ledgerline under
the category "Home Office".

## 6. Learning and Growth

Every employee has a personal **learning budget of €1,800 per fiscal year**, plus **5 learning
days**. The budget covers courses, conferences, books, and certifications. Unused budget does not
carry over. Spending above €600 on a single item needs manager approval in Ledgerline.

Brightmoor runs an internal mentoring programme, **Lighthouse**, which pairs every new joiner with a
mentor from a different department for their first 6 months. Engineers also take part in **Forge
Fridays**: the last Friday of every month is reserved for self-directed learning, tooling
improvements, and experiments.

Performance reviews, called **Tide Reviews**, happen twice a year, in **April** and **October**.
Salary adjustments are made once a year, effective **1 May**. Promotions can be proposed in either
review cycle and are decided by a calibration committee chaired by the Chief People Officer,
Lena Marchetti.

## 7. IT and Security

### 7.1 Devices

Engineers receive a 16-inch laptop; everyone else receives a 14-inch laptop. Laptops are replaced
every **3 years**, or sooner if repair would cost more than 40% of a new device. Personal devices
may access email and chat only through the managed **Beacon** app; source code and customer data
must never be stored on personal devices.

### 7.2 Accounts and passwords

Every account uses single sign-on with a hardware security key. Each employee receives **two
YubiKeys** on their first day: one for daily use and one kept at home as a backup. SMS-based
multi-factor authentication is not allowed for any company system. Lost keys must be reported
within **4 hours** to the IT service desk.

### 7.3 Reporting security incidents

Suspected security incidents, including phishing, lost devices, and leaked credentials, are reported
immediately in the `#sec-alarm` channel or by calling the security hotline on **+372 5550 1177**
(internal extension **7117**). The Head of Security is Oskar Järvinen. Never try to investigate a
suspected compromise yourself, and never delete a suspicious email before security has seen it.

Data is classified in four levels: **Public**, **Internal**, **Confidential**, and **Restricted**.
Customer warehouse maps and robot telemetry are always **Restricted**, which means they may only be
stored in the EU-hosted **Fathom** data platform and must never be pasted into external AI tools.

## 8. On-call and Incident Management

Customer-facing production systems are covered 24/7 by an on-call rotation. Rotations last **one
week**, starting on **Monday at 09:00 Tallinn time**. Nobody is scheduled for on-call more than one
week in every five. On-call engineers receive **€35 per weekday** and **€70 per weekend day or
public holiday** as an on-call allowance, plus time off in lieu for any call-out longer than 2 hours.

Incidents are classified by severity:

| Severity | Definition | Response time | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEV1 | A customer site is fully stopped, or there is a safety risk | 10 minutes | Kestrel fleet halted at a customer warehouse |
| SEV2 | Major degradation with a workaround | 30 minutes | Pick rate below 60% of normal |
| SEV3 | Minor issue, no customer impact yet | Next business day | Dashboard delayed by more than 1 hour |

For every SEV1, the incident commander opens a channel named `#inc-<date>-<short-name>` and
notifies the customer within **30 minutes**. A blameless post-incident review, called a **Tidemark**,
must be published within **5 working days** of any SEV1 or SEV2.

## 9. Benefits

- **Health insurance:** Private health insurance through our provider covers employees and their
  children. Partners can be added at 50% of the premium.
- **Pension:** Brightmoor matches pension contributions up to **5% of base salary**.
- **Wellbeing allowance:** €50 per month, paid with salary, for sport, therapy, or wellbeing apps.
- **Commuting:** A public transport pass or bike lease is covered up to **€60 per month**.
- **Equity:** All permanent employees receive stock options after their probation period. Options
  vest over **four years** with a **one-year cliff**.
- **Sabbatical:** After **seven years** of service, employees can take a **six-week paid
  sabbatical**, to be used within the following 24 months.

The probation period is **4 months** for all new employees. Notice periods are 1 month during
probation and 2 months afterwards (3 months for managers).

## 10. Code of Conduct and Speaking Up

We expect everyone to act in line with Candour, Craft, and Care. Harassment, discrimination, and
retaliation are never tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, with People Operations,
or anonymously through **Signal Flare**, our independent whistleblowing line, which is operated by
an external provider and available 24/7.

Gifts from suppliers or customers worth more than **€75** must be declared in Harbour within
**7 days**. Gifts worth more than €250 must be politely declined. Conflicts of interest, including
side projects in the robotics or logistics industry, must be declared to your manager and to the
General Counsel, Priya Raman-Castell.
